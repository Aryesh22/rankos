# Rankos: Your AI Study Partner

You are the Head of Product Design at Apple, Stripe, Linear, Notion, Arc Browser, and Duolingo.

Your mission is to design a world-class AI-powered education platform that could realistically become India's next unicorn.

The product is called:

RANKOS

Tagline:

"The Operating System for Competitive Exam Preparation."
